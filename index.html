Introduction

“Impend” is an original animated music video created by Hydrocivik/Hyrax-x, who composed and produced the music, and handled animation and writing. This project is submitted as the Diploma requirement for the Bachelor of Arts (BA) in Graphic Design and Multimedia.

Guided by three supervisors: Anna Eichler (Main), Mariusz Jarmulski (Technical), and Paulina Duda (Theoretical), “Impend” integrates music, narrative, and animation, reflecting the culmination of interdisciplinary study and artistic vision.

Movie

Story

“Impend” follows a resilient cat surviving in a post-apocalyptic city overrun by a giant, laser-shooting alien. The cat continues its daily ritual of bringing food to its deceased owner. When a mouse steals the offering, the cat chases it through the ruined city. In a moment of despair, the cat discovers a hidden laser power and uses it to fight back against the alien invaders.

Music

Artist

Hydrocivik is a multi-instrumentalist with seven years of experience in piano, drums, and guitar. Specializing in EDM, chill instrumentals, and piano pieces, Hydrocivik now aims to create music with greater depth.

Music

“Impend” combines orchestral, metal, and melodic dubstep elements with Vocaloid vocals to create a dynamic soundscape. Developed over two years, the track demonstrates an intricate fusion of genres, highlighting both emotional depth and innovative production.
